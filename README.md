# Customer_Behavior_Analysis
📌 Project OverviewThis end-to-end data analytics project analyzes customer shopping behavior across 3,900 transactional records spanning multiple product categories. The objective is to uncover actionable insights into spending patterns, customer demographics, product preferences, discount sensitivity, and subscription behavior to guide strategic business decisions.   📂 Dataset SummaryTotal Records: 3,900 rows and 18 columns.   Customer Demographics: Customer ID, Age, Gender, Location, and Subscription Status.   Purchase Details: Item Purchased, Category (Clothing, Accessories, Footwear, Outerwear), Purchase Amount (USD), Season, Size, and Color.   Shopping Behavior: Discount Applied, Promo Code Used, Previous Purchases, Frequency of Purchases, Review Rating, Payment Method, and Shipping Type.   Data Quality Note: Contained 37 missing values exclusively in the Review Rating column prior to cleaning.   🛠️ Tech Stack & WorkflowPython (pandas, sqlalchemy, psycopg2): Data cleaning, missing-value imputation, feature engineering, and automated database ingestion.   PostgreSQL (pgAdmin 4): Relational data storage and complex SQL querying (CTEs, Window Functions, Conditional Aggregations) to answer 10 core business questions.   Power BI Desktop: Interactive dashboard design featuring dynamic slicers and KPI cards.   🧹 1. Exploratory Data Analysis & Data Cleaning (Python)Missing Data Imputation: Imputed the 37 null values in Review Rating using the median rating of each respective product category (df.groupby('Category')['Review Rating'].transform(...)).   Schema Standardization: Converted all column headers to snake_case and renamed purchase_amount_(usd) to purchase_amount for seamless SQL compatibility.   Feature Engineering:Engineered age_group (Young Adult, Adult, Middle-aged, Senior) using quartile binning (pd.qcut).   Mapped text-based purchase intervals into a numerical purchase_frequency_days metric (e.g., Weekly = 7, Fortnightly/Bi-Weekly = 14, Monthly = 30, Quarterly/Every 3 Months = 90, Annually = 365).   Redundancy Removal: Verified 100% equivalence between discount_applied and promo_code_used (True across all rows) and dropped promo_code_used.   Database Pipeline: Loaded the cleaned 19-column DataFrame directly into PostgreSQL (customer_behavior table) via SQLAlchemy.   🗄️ 2. SQL Business Analysis & Key Findings (PostgreSQL)We executed 10 structured SQL queries to extract key business metrics:   Revenue by Gender: Male customers generated $157,890 in total revenue compared to $75,191 from female customers.   High-Spending Discount Users: Identified 839 customers who applied discounts while still spending above the overall average purchase amount ($59.76).   Top 5 Products by Review Rating: Gloves (3.86), Sandals (3.84), Boots (3.82), Hat (3.80), and Skirt (3.78) achieved the highest customer satisfaction ratings.   Shipping Type Comparison: Customers choosing Express Shipping spent more on average ($60.48) than those selecting Standard Shipping ($58.46).   Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers: Non-subscribers accounted for 2,847 customers ($170,436 total revenue; $59.87 avg spend), while subscribers accounted for 1,053 customers ($62,645 total revenue; $59.49 avg spend).   Discount-Dependent Products: Hat (50.00%), Sneakers (49.66%), Coat (49.07%), Sweater (48.17%), and Pants (47.37%) had the highest share of discounted transactions.   Customer Segmentation: Using SQL CTEs on previous_purchases, customers were classified into Loyal (3,116 customers), Returning (701 customers), and New (83 customers) segments.   Top 3 Products per Category: Ranked top-selling items using ROW_NUMBER() OVER(PARTITION BY category...)—led by Jewelry in Accessories (171 orders), Blouse & Pants in Clothing (171 orders each), Sandals in Footwear (160 orders), and Jacket in Outerwear (163 orders).   Repeat Buyers & Subscription Uptake: Among repeat buyers with more than 5 previous purchases, 2,518 are non-subscribers while 958 are active subscribers, signaling a major conversion opportunity.   Revenue by Age Group: Young Adults led revenue contribution ($62,143), followed by Middle-aged ($59,197), Adults ($55,978), and Seniors ($55,763).   📊 3. Power BI DashboardAn interactive Customer Behavior Dashboard was developed in Power BI featuring:   Executive KPI Cards: 3.9K Total Customers, $59.76 Average Purchase Amount, and 3.75 Average Review Rating.   Interactive Slicers: Filter dynamically by Subscription Status, Gender, Category, and Shipping Type.   Visual Breakdowns: Donut chart of subscription split (27% Yes vs. 73% No), column charts of Revenue and Sales by Category, and bar charts tracking Revenue and Sales across Age Groups.   💡 Strategic Business RecommendationsBoost Subscription Conversions: Launch exclusive subscriber perks targeted at the 2,518 repeat buyers (>5 purchases) who have not yet subscribed.   Tiered Customer Loyalty Programs: Incentivize the 701 Returning shoppers with milestone rewards to transition them into the high-retention Loyal tier.   Optimize Discount Policy: Audit profit margins on heavily discounted items (such as Hats, Sneakers, and Coats, where ~49–50% of sales use discounts) to balance volume growth with profitability.   Targeted Marketing & Product Positioning: Spotlight top-rated items (Gloves, Sandals, Boots) and focus ad spend on high-revenue Young Adult and Middle-aged demographics as well as Express Shipping shoppers.
